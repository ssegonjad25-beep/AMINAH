Explore Africa's incredible wildlife

Africa is famous for its diverse wildlife and beautiful natural habitats. Many visitors travel to national parks and wildlife reserves to observe wild animals in their natural surroundings.

African lion

Lions are large wild cats known for their strength and social behaviour. They live in parts of sub-Saharan Africa, including grassland habitats.

African elephant

African elephants are the largest living land animals. These intelligent mammals play an important role in maintaining their environments.

Protect African wildlife

Wildlife conservation helps protect animals and their habitats for future generations.

  • Protect national parks and wildlife reserves.
  • Prevent illegal wildlife trade.
  • Protect forests and natural habitats.

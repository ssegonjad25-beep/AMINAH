African culture

Introduction to African culture

Africa is a continent with many different cultures, languages, traditions and communities. Each community has its own history and customs, making the continent a fascinating place to learn about.

Traditional music and dance

Music and dance are important parts of many African celebrations. People use drums, songs and traditional dances during festivals, weddings and other occasions.

Traditional clothing

African clothing varies across regions and communities. Fabrics and garments reflect local identity, history and creative traditions.

African foods

African countries have many traditional foods, including matooke in Uganda, injera in Ethiopia, jollof rice in parts of West Africa and couscous in North Africa.

Respecting African culture

  • Respect local customs and traditions.
  • Ask permission before taking photographs of people.
  • Listen respectfully and learn from local communities.

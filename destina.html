Discover amazing destinations

Africa is home to wonderful tourist attractions, including mountains, waterfalls, national parks, deserts, beaches and historical landmarks.

Uganda

Murchison Falls National Park

Murchison Falls is one of Uganda's well-known tourist destinations, offering wildlife viewing and beautiful scenery.

Tanzania

Mount Kilimanjaro

Africa's highest mountain attracts visitors who enjoy mountain climbing and exploring dramatic natural landscapes.

Kenya

Maasai Mara National Reserve

The reserve is known for its wildlife, open grasslands and the annual wildebeest migration.

South Africa

Cape Town

Cape Town is known for Table Mountain, beautiful beaches, scenic views and a variety of tourist activities.
